Phishing IR Quick Playbook (PICERL aligned)

  1. Identify: Validate indicators (headers, URL, attachment, authentication failures).
  2. Contain: Quarantine message, block sender/domain, consider ZAP for widespread delivery.
  3. Evidence: Preserve headers, malicious URL, hash, recipient list, and timeline.
  4. Eradicate: Remove remaining artifacts, deny related domains/hashes, tune detection.
  5. Recover: Verify mailbox restoration and user account integrity.
  6. Lessons Learned: Record root cause and update playbooks/rules.
